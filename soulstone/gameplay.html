Gameplay

Start of round

  1. At the start of each round, all players discard unwanted cards from their cheat hand and shuffle their deck. Each player then draws new cards until their hand is full (7 cards)
  2. All players roll a D6 initiativ. The player with the highest roll then descides if they want to go first or if the next player next in line gets the option
  3. Alternating around the table, each player activates one of their heroes until all heroes has performed their three actions

Neutral actions

Move The hero moves up to its movement speed on the horizontal plane. Some heroes have keyword climber which allows the hero to scale large terrain by using movement speed
Climb up / Drop down The hero moves up and down larger terrain pieces such as houses
Harvest soulstone Reduces a soulstones harvest value by 1. If the value is reduced to 0, the soulstone is picked up and placed in the heroes inventory

Combat actions

Basic attack All heroes have a basic attack available whether it is shooting with a bow or hitting someone with their fists. No skill level is added to the duel for basic attacks
Use abilities Triggering an ability following the specified effect on the hero card. Each ability explanation starts with the frequency it can be used
React A hero can activate an ability with the keyword reaction even if it's not their turn if one of the following is true:
  • The hero has not had their activation turn yet this round. When it's this hero's turn to activate later in the turn, it has one less available action to take
  • The hero passed on one of their 3 allowed actions, saving one of them for a reaction
