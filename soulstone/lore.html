Lore

For as long as time itself, creatures of all kinds have gazed upon the world around them and wondered at the nature of their existence. Is the sky above them the only sky? Are the stars distant lights in an endless void, or are they windows into worlds beyond their comprehension? And when life comes to an end, does the soul simply fade into nothingness, or does it return to something greater? For most, such questions have remained little more than philosophical musings, matters left to scholars, prophets, and the sleepless minds of those who dare to look beyond the horizon of their own reality. But for the citizens of NAME, the answers have become painfully, terrifyingly clear.

There is more to existence than any single world could ever contain. Countless realities stretch beyond the boundaries of what most would consider possible, each with its own laws, histories, civilizations, and horrors. Some worlds flourish beneath eternal suns, while others languish in perpetual darkness. Some are governed by the laws of science, others by forces their inhabitants call magic. And somewhere within this endless expanse, countless civilizations have lived and died, never knowing that their reality was but one fragment of something far greater. Until the boundaries began to fall. It started with an event that would later be remembered by many names, though none could truly capture the magnitude of what had occurred. To some, it was the Great Convergence. To others, the Shattering. Whatever its name, its consequences were the same: the barriers separating realities began to dissolve. Without warning, the city of NAME became the epicenter of a catastrophe beyond mortal understanding. Streets that had stood for centuries twisted into unfamiliar shapes. Buildings from distant worlds emerged between familiar structures, their foundations buried beneath foreign soil. Skies bled into one another, revealing alien constellations, impossible moons, and landscapes that should never have existed beneath the same heavens. Entire districts were swallowed by the geography of other realities. Ancient ruins surfaced in the middle of bustling marketplaces. Forests of impossible flora consumed neighborhoods overnight, while rivers flowed uphill and gravity itself became an unreliable law. In some places, the dead walked beneath unfamiliar stars. In others, time fractured, trapping unfortunate souls in moments that repeated endlessly. And through it all, the rifts remained open. What had once been separate worlds were now colliding, their inhabitants forced into an existence where nothing could be taken for granted. Soldiers found themselves fighting alongside creatures from their nightmares. Refugees wandered through streets populated by beings who spoke unfamiliar languages and followed customs beyond comprehension. Some sought shelter, others answered, and many simply sought to survive. But survival would prove to be only the beginning.

The collapse of the boundaries between realms did not merely bring different worlds together. It ignited a war. As the first shockwaves of the catastrophe subsided, the inhabitants of these fractured realities began to recognize both the opportunity and the threat before them. Ancient rivalries resurfaced. Empires saw new territories ripe for conquest. Religious orders declared the event a divine judgment, while ambitious rulers proclaimed it their destiny to unite the broken worlds beneath their banners. Alliances formed and shattered with alarming speed. Some fought to restore the boundaries between realities, desperate to return to the homes they had lost. Others embraced the chaos, believing that the destruction of the old order was the first step toward something greater. And still others cared little for the fate of the multiverse, seeing only the chance to seize power in a world where the impossible had become commonplace. Thus began the multiversal civil war, a conflict in which the very meaning of home, nation, and reality was thrown into question. Yet, amid the bloodshed and destruction, something extraordinary descended upon the city. From the deepest fabric of existence, vast crystalline formations broke free. They fell from the fractured skies like fragments of creation itself, crashing into the ruined streets and embedding themselves in the earth. Their surfaces shimmered with colors that had no names, and their inner depths seemed to contain entire galaxies, swirling with faint, distant lights. Where these crystals landed, the dying found no peace. The fallen were drawn toward them, their life essence torn from their bodies and pulled into the crystalline depths. Warriors, civilians, monsters, and beings from realms beyond mortal comprehension all met the same fate. Their final breaths became motes of light, their memories flickered across translucent surfaces, and the remnants of their existence were absorbed into the stones.

At first, these phenomena were mistaken for another manifestation of the catastrophe. Some feared the crystals were parasites, feeding upon the suffering of the city. Others worshipped them as divine relics, believing they had been sent to judge the living. Many simply avoided them, unwilling to approach objects that seemed to grow more radiant with every death. It was only when the scholars arrived that the true significance of the crystals began to emerge. Drawn from countless realities, these scholars, arcanists, scientists, and philosophers began to study the strange formations. Their discoveries would change the course of the war forever. The crystals, soon named Soulstones, were not merely artifacts born from the collapse of the realms. They were something far more fundamental. They were fragments of the very structure upon which existence depended. According to the theories that emerged from their research, every universe was sustained by an intricate balance of forces, an invisible architecture binding matter, energy, time, and life into a coherent reality. The Soulstones were remnants of this architecture, crystallized pieces of the primordial foundation from which worlds were formed. They contained traces of the forces that shaped creation itself, along with the life essence of those who had fallen in their presence. More astonishing still was the discovery that a Soulstone could be used. Those who managed to claim one found themselves capable of wielding powers that defied the natural laws of their homeworlds. Some could bend the elements to their will, while others manipulated space, distorted time, or channeled energies previously thought to exist only in myth. The precise abilities granted by a Soulstone varied, seemingly influenced by the nature of the stone, the individual who possessed it, and the essence contained within. But power was only part of the revelation. The scholars soon discovered that Soulstones could also stabilize the fabric of a reality. A world whose foundations had begun to weaken could be sustained by the presence of these crystalline fragments, slowing the decay of its natural laws and protecting it from the encroaching chaos beyond its borders. In the wake of the Convergence, countless worlds were already beginning to unravel. Their skies fractured, their landscapes warped, and the rules governing their existence grew increasingly unstable. For some, the collapse was gradual. For others, entire civilizations faced extinction within days. The Soulstones offered a chance to prevent that fate. To possess one was to wield extraordinary power. To possess enough of them might mean the difference between the survival and destruction of an entire world. And so, the war changed. What had begun as a desperate struggle for survival became a race for the very building blocks of existence. The scattered Soulstones transformed the ruined city of NAME into the most contested territory in the known multiverse. Every fallen crystal became a prize worth armies, every battlefield a potential source of unimaginable power, and every expedition into the fractured districts an opportunity to secure the future of an entire civilization.

The realms that had once feared the Convergence began sending reinforcements through the open rifts. Armies marched beneath banners from distant dimensions. Mercenaries, treasure hunters, cultists, and exiles followed in their wake, each driven by their own ambitions. Some came to protect their people. Others sought wealth, glory, or revenge. A few hoped to uncover the truth behind the catastrophe itself. The city became a crossroads of impossible encounters and unending conflict, where ancient magic clashed with advanced technology, where forgotten gods could walk beside mortal soldiers, and where the fate of entire universes might be decided over a single shard of crystal. Yet, beneath the greed and ambition, one question continued to haunt those who understood what the Soulstones truly represented. If these crystals were fragments of the fabric of existence, then why had they broken free? Was the Convergence an accident, a natural consequence of forces beyond comprehension? Has something deliberately torn the boundaries between realities apart? And if the Soulstones were capable of stabilizing the worlds they came from, what would happen if too many were taken from the place where they had fallen?

Perhaps the crystals were the key to saving the multiverse. Perhaps they were the only thing keeping it from collapsing entirely. Or perhaps the war itself was bringing about something far worse than anyone had yet imagined. For now, the rifts remain open. The armies continue to arrive. The Soulstones continue to call to those who seek their power. And in the fractured city of NAME, where countless realities have been forced to share the same broken streets, one truth has become undeniable: The fate of entire worlds rests in the hands of those willing to fight for it.
