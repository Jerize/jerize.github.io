Combat effects

The following table shows the different combat effects

Stunned During the next activation, this character can't use any Neutral moves
Slowed During the next activation, this character has their Movement speeds reduced to 2"
Slowed During the next activation, this character can't trigger an ability targeting an enemy(friendly targets can still be chosen)
Advantage During the next duel this hero has advantage resulting in two drawn cards instead of one. The desired card is then picked by the player with advantage
Disadvantage During the next duel this hero has advantage resulting in two drawn cards instead of one. The desired card is then picked by the player your hero is in duel with

Reaction abilities

Some characters have their own reaction abilities with their own rules, however all characters can pick one additional reaction ability out of a couple available. Each team can only pick one of each ability for their team

Heal Instantly return up to x health to your hero
Ghost Increase movement speed by 100% this activation
Barrier Gain x extra magic resistance, physical resistance and armor this activation
Teleport Pick a friendly unit and teleport to them, the hero must be placed with base contact of the targeted friendly unit
Smite Instantly reduce the harvest counter of the targeted soulstone by 2. Must be in range of the soulstone chosen

Terrain

Some characters have their own reaction abilities with their own rules, however all characters can pick one additional reaction ability out of a couple available. Each team can only pick one of each ability for their team

Terrain Attacking an hero using cover gives the attacking hero duel disadvantage
High ground Attacking from high ground gives the attacking hero duel advantage
Rough terrain Slowing the hero down, reducing the speed to 50%.

Infused Soulstones

As the name implies, soulstones can also carry souls from fallen heroes to give the owner a magical boost. If a hero (friendly or enemy) dies within 2” of a hero carrying a soulstone, that stone gets infused by the hero's soul (limited to one per stone). Using the stone removes the frequency cooldown on any ability, consuming the soul in the process
