The Goal

As the ruler of a world caught in the chaos of the Convergence, your fate rests upon the Soulstones scattered throughout the fractured city. These crystalline fragments hold the power to strengthen your armies, reshape the battlefield, and stabilize the very foundations of your homeworld. But you are not alone in seeking them. Rival rulers and their forces will stop at nothing to claim these precious resources for themselves. Your objective is simple: claim as many Soulstones as possible before your enemies do.

The battle unfolds over four chaotic rounds, as rival forces clash, alliances are tested, and the fate of entire worlds hangs in the balance. Every Soulstone you secure brings you one step closer to victory, granting your world greater power and a chance at survival.

Choose your battles wisely, outmaneuver your rivals, and seize every opportunity you can. When the dust settles, the ruler who has gathered the most Soulstones will have secured a precious advantage in the struggle for the multiverse's future.

In this war, power belongs to those who can claim it.

Victory Conditions

  • Duration: 4 rounds.
  • Victory: The player who holds the most Soulstones at the end of the fourth round wins.
