King Jarvan IV

"I do not ask my knights to die for my kingdom. I ask them to live for it, to protect it, and to remember what they stand for."

King Jarvan IV is the reigning monarch of Atherion and the embodiment of its ideals. A firm but fair ruler, he believes that the strength of a kingdom is measured not by the wealth of its crown, but by the safety and prosperity of its people. His rule is guided by a simple principle: no one, not even the nobility, stands above the law.

Jarvan maintains close ties with the kingdom's knightly orders and military commanders, often taking a personal interest in matters concerning the defence of the realm. He is respected by his soldiers and subjects alike, though his commitment to justice can sometimes place him at odds with powerful nobles who would prefer a more favourable interpretation of the law.

Under his reign, Atherion has become a place where commoners can rise through merit, where service is rewarded, and where even the lowliest adventurer might earn an audience with the crown through exceptional deeds.

A King's Burden

The disappearance of his brother has left a lasting mark on Jarvan.

Though he rarely speaks of his brother, those closest to him know that the matter remains deeply personal. Some believe his commitment to maintaining the connection between Atherion and Gravemourn stems not only from duty, but from the hope that his brother might still be found.

He has quietly funded expeditions and investigations into the world, though none have produced any conclusive answers. Sona is among the few people aware of the extent of his efforts. She has repeatedly cautioned him against allowing personal grief to compromise his judgement. Jarvan, however, remains convinced that until there is proof of his brother's death, there is still reason to search.

"A kingdom may mourn its fallen, but I will not bury my brother on the word of those who never saw him fall."
