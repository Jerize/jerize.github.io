Sona - The Royal Hand

"You need not be fearless to stand your ground. You need only remember why you stand there in the first place."

Among the most trusted individuals in King Jarvan IV's court is Sona, his right hand and the principal educator of Atherion's knights. Though she does not hold the title of queen, her influence within the kingdom is considerable. She serves as the king's closest military advisor, overseeing the training, discipline and development of those who swear their lives to the crown.

Sona is a respected figure throughout Atherion, known for her wisdom, patience and exceptional understanding of both warfare and the knightly virtues. Where Jarvan represents the authority and justice of the crown, Sona represents the ideals that its knights are expected to uphold. She believes that a knight's greatest strength is not found in their sword arm, but in their ability to know when to draw their weapon and, more importantly, when to sheath it.

Sona's most distinctive possession is an enchanted horn, an artefact of extraordinary magical properties. Its origins are uncertain, with some believing it to be a relic from an age long before Atherion's founding. The horn is more than a weapon. It is an instrument of blessing, capable of channeling powerful magic through its wielder and those around her. When sounded, its melodies carry an almost tangible presence across the battlefield. Those who hear its call often describe an overwhelming sense of clarity, courage and renewed determination. Wounds seem less debilitating, exhaustion becomes easier to overcome, and even the most frightened soldiers find the strength to stand their ground.

The Price of Harmony

Few know the true nature of Sona's enchanted horn. To most, it is a sacred artefact, a symbol of hope and an instrument capable of turning the tide of even the bleakest battles. But its magic comes at a price.

The horn does not possess an endless source of power. Instead, it draws upon the life force of its wielder, converting her vitality into the blessings it bestows upon others. The greater the magic invoked, the greater the toll it takes. Every melody, every protective blessing and every life saved slowly consumes something within Sona.

At first, the effects were barely noticeable. A moment of exhaustion after battle, a faint tremor in her hands or an unusual pallor that she attributed to sleepless nights. Over the years, however, the symptoms have become increasingly difficult to ignore. Sona suspects that one day, the horn will take more than she can afford to give.

"A blessing is never truly free. Someone, somewhere, must bear its cost."
