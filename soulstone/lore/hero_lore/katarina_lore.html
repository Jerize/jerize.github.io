Katarina - The Shadow of Gravemourn

Long before Katarina became known as the The Shadow of Gravemourn, she was something quite different. She was once a mortal who crossed into Gravemourn, though the circumstances surrounding her arrival remain largely unknown. Unlike most who enter the world of death, Katarina did not perish, nor did she become one of the Forgotten. Instead, she adapted.

The shadows of Gravemourn became an extension of her body, allowing her to move between them almost instantaneously. She learned to traverse the world through its darkness, striking with her daggers before her victims could even comprehend her presence. But Gravemourn changed more than her abilities. It bound her to something ancient, something that had long been dormant.
